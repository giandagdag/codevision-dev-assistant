## The shape of the change (in AS400 words)

Understand today is like a 5250 program that shows one question per screen, and after each answer
may show an extra "decision" screen. The fix is one input screen with 4 required fields and 1
optional field (like a DDS record format with 5 fields), one validation pass, and only then the
extra decision screens. Everything else is wording, button labels and two auto-chained actions.
